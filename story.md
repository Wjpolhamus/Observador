Sometimes we need to sit back, relax and observe our surroundings. Observador is intended to be a game with no rush, where the player can go at their own pace, 
and explore the beautiful world. The game will award those who observe and pay attention, but won't penalize you if you don't choose that path. The storys intention is 
to teach the player that it is worth it to pay attention, and often that is what we are missing in life. When life is filled with anxieties and fears, sometimes
you need something that can guide you and help you better understand the world around you.

This game is one of my creative outlets. I have always wanted to create something, and while I have many things I want to make - this allows me to create a story where 
people can view it and enjoy, and hopefully become inspired by. I live my life trying my best to bring kindness and understanding, and this is my way to convey my personality
into art. But unfortunately within the gaming community there are so many games to try. So there is a lot to learn, like how to make your game stand out, or how to 
create a compelling story to hook the player.

I am attempting to create this with my own art, own story. I want to create a game that users remember, like Undertale, Pokemon, Earthbound, Stardew valley, etc. And
while it is really difficult as the field is over-saturated - I will try my best to create something original that will leave an impression on the player.
